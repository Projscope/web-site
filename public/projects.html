MeshDiff

MeshDiff

A web-based SaaS for hardware and 3D-printing teams to see exactly what changed between two versions of a model — and whether the new one will actually print. A voxel + SDF diff engine highlights added/removed geometry and dimensional drift, backed by a wall-thickness/overhang/unsupported-area printability check, 3D-coordinate-pinned comments, and a full approval workflow (Draft → In Review → Approved) for teams. The core diff tool stays free, no account needed.
